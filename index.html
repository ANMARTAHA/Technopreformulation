Overview

The essential rules and ideas of preformulation — no lecture, just the core facts you need to remember.

DEFINITION

What preformulation is

Determining the intrinsic and derived physicochemical properties of a new chemical entity — the stage that must happen before proper formulation can begin.

PROPERTIES

Intrinsic vs derived

Intrinsic properties belong to the molecule and change only by chemical modification. Derived (bulk) properties come from intermolecular interactions and depend on solid-state form.

WORKFLOW

Assay first

No property can be measured without a validated assay — assay development is always the first preformulation step.

TOOLKIT

The core five techniques

UV spectrophotometry, HPLC, DSC, DVS and XRPD are enough to fully characterize most candidates. TLC and TGA are useful, not essential.

SOLUBILITY

The 10 / 1 mg mL⁻¹ rule

Above 10 mg/mL is preferable for an oral solid. Below 1 mg/mL, salt formation is indicated; if that fails, a novel dosage form is required.

SOLUBILITY

Intrinsic solubility (S₀)

The equilibrium solubility of the un-ionized form of an ionizable drug — the baseline value used in all pH-solubility calculations.

THERMODYNAMICS

ΔHsol = ΔHf + ΔHmix

Dissolution enthalpy is the sum of the enthalpy needed to break the crystal lattice (fusion) and the enthalpy of mixing with solvent.

TEMPERATURE

Endothermic vs exothermic

ΔHsol positive (endothermic): solubility rises with temperature. ΔHsol negative (exothermic): solubility falls with temperature.

SOLID STATE

Polymorph solubility order

Amorphous > metastable polymorph > stable polymorph, because stable forms have the highest melting point and ΔHf.

IONIZATION

The ⅔ rule

About two-thirds of marketed drugs ionize between pH 2 and pH 12 — acid/base behaviour is central to preformulation.

pH RULE

±2 pH-unit rule

2 units below pKa: a weak acid is essentially un-ionized. 2 units above pKa: it is essentially fully ionized (reverse for a base).

MEASUREMENT

pKa from a titration

Potentiometric titration; the point where pH = pKa is where the compound is exactly 50% ionized.

PARTITIONING

Only the un-ionized form partitions

Ionized species are too polar for the organic phase, so log P only strictly applies when the drug is un-ionized.

LOG P

Sign tells the story

P > 1 (log P positive) = lipophilic. P < 1 (log P negative) = hydrophilic. n-Octanol models the lipid bilayer.
